## Uh... Who's Bob?
As you may have guessed stumbling across this site, Bob isn't a who but rather a what. Bob is a red banana plant 
currently being grown in the expansive maple syrup producing region of the world – Canada.

## Why the Name Bob?
Well you see Bob is actually an acronym for Big 'Ol' Banana. More specifically Bob is a *Ensete 
ventricosum* commonly referred to by the following names: Abyssinian banana, pseudo-banana, and wild banana, among 
others.

## What's Bob's Story?
Bob's story is developing right now. Starting in 2026, this site will be a place to log, share, and record 
Bob's growth and development. Along the way we'll go bananas for bananas learning (together) plenty of tasty facts and
sundry about Bob.

Check back regularly for more updates regarding all things Bob. Thanks for stopping by and have a 
bananlicious day!
