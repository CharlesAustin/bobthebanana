Welcome to the banana-dome! Two plants enter, one plant leaves! Just kidding, *maybe*.

This is the inaugural post for our subject, Bob the Banana. So here's the deal: we're going to be tracking the
progress of Bob's journey over the summer of 2026 and (possibly) the rest of his life. This summer we're going all
in on bananas, botany, and backyard bliss, *whether you like it or not* (I'm assuming you do like it as you're still
reading this).

Each week this blog will be updated (maybe more than once) with a new banana update on Bob. Depending on how far we get
into the banana-verse, there might be supplemental material sprinkled here and there to grow our knowledge of all things
green-thumb.

In other words, check in regularly because we are going to grow, show, and bestow.
